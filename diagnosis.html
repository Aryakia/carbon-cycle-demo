Structural revision queue

Pre-declared hypotheses for the next model version.

1 · Generation, not capacity alone

Test renewable/low-carbon electricity generation share as the clean-competition measurement variable. Installed GW does not directly equal delivered fossil displacement.

2 · Efficiency and electrification

Separate final energy services, electrification and conversion efficiency rather than forcing all decline into one fossil-dependency state.

3 · Fuel and sector heterogeneity

Coal, oil and gas face different substitution processes and turnover. A global aggregate can mask different causal structures.

4 · Regional/policy heterogeneity

Use observable regional transition and policy variables. Do not insert a fitted “post-2017” dummy after seeing the residuals.

New validation protocol

The old holdout cannot be recycled.

Internal model development

Use pre-declared blocked rolling-origin folds: 2000–09→2010–13; 2000–13→2014–17; 2000–17→2018–21; 2000–21→2022–25.

External validation

Reserve a separate regional/fuel-sector panel or future global data after 2025. Revised structures motivated by Stage 15 must never describe 2018–2025 as untouched validation.
