Carbon is not just a bathtub.

Move through time and watch atmospheric CO₂ fill the bathtub. Then look upstream and downstream: fossil carbon enters from a very large geological stock, while land and ocean remove part of the flow into active reservoirs. A slow geological return path closes the conceptual loop without pretending that natural sinks rapidly rebuild fossil stocks.

System Dynamics takeaway

The leverage point is the transfer rate, not exhaustion of the upstream stock.

The atmosphere is a comparatively small stock. A geologically modest transfer can therefore create a large concentration change, while natural sinks redistribute part of that carbon into land and ocean reservoirs rather than instantly returning it to fossil storage.
