# Model positioning: Carbon Beyond the Bathtub, EN-ROADS, and EPS

## Carbon Beyond the Bathtub

The present project has a narrow research/teaching focus:

> Why does fossil-carbon mobilization persist, and how can historical investment, infrastructure, capability, institutional support, and policy delays mediate the path from policy to extraction and atmospheric carbon?

Its advanced engine explicitly includes a fuel-inventory step between extraction and use, non-combustion product carbon, production-capacity and fossil-demand-capital stocks, clean capital, capability, and institutional support.

It is currently an **uncalibrated behavioral-hypothesis model** coupled to tested carbon-accounting identities.

## EN-ROADS

The current official EN-ROADS technical reference describes a globally aggregated system-dynamics model of energy, economy, land use, agriculture, greenhouse gases, and climate. It is calibrated to extensive historical data and informed by other large models. EN-ROADS includes endogenous energy supply/demand, capital, learning, resource depletion, greenhouse-gas cycles, climate, land use, and other processes.

Official reference:
https://docs.climateinteractive.org/projects/en-roads-reference-guide/en/latest/

Important distinction: EN-ROADS is much broader and more mature. Carbon Beyond the Bathtub should not reproduce that breadth. Its proposed value is a simpler, inspectable upstream-lock-in chain and educational metaphor.

## Energy Policy Simulator (EPS)

The official EPS documentation describes a free/open-source policy model with major economy sectors including:

- transportation;
- electricity supply;
- buildings;
- industry;
- agriculture;
- land use.

EPS is designed to evaluate many policies and includes emissions, financial, and public-health outcomes.

Official documentation:
https://docs.energypolicy.solutions/

Important distinction: Carbon Beyond the Bathtub is not currently sectorally detailed and does not produce defensible economy-wide costs or health outcomes.

## Intended complementarity

The project should be positioned as a transparent explanatory / hypothesis-building layer that can be used alongside mature simulators:

- use the bathtub and carbon ledger for stock-flow learning;
- use the upstream laboratory to expose hypothesized mediating mechanisms;
- use experiments to discuss capital turnover, timing, institutional resistance, and historical initial conditions;
- use EN-ROADS or EPS when a broader calibrated policy-system analysis is required.

This positioning avoids unsupported novelty claims and makes the scientific boundary explicit.
