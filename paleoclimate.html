What the comparison means: late-Pleistocene CO₂ rose and fell naturally with glacial–interglacial carbon-cycle changes, but the modern anthropogenic perturbation is occurring from an already high interglacial baseline and has pushed atmospheric CO₂ well beyond the preindustrial range represented by the ice-core composite. This figure does not attribute every ancient change to one process and does not treat natural sinks as a fixed-capacity drain.

Do not collapse these categories

Resource abundance is not a carbon budget.

  • Resources are not proved reserves.
  • Resources are not all geological carbon.
  • Physical occurrence is not economic recoverability.
  • Extraction is not the same event as combustion or atmospheric emission.
  • A large resource stock does not imply that it will—or safely can—enter the atmosphere.

Modern comparison

Global Carbon Budget 2025

The 2025 budget reports 422.80 ± 0.1 ppm and about 898 GtC in the atmosphere in 2024, compared with about 278 ppm and 590 GtC around 1750. It also states that the current atmospheric CO₂ increase is at least ten times faster than any other time during the last 800,000 years.
