Systems insight

Emissions are flows; atmospheric CO₂ is an accumulated outcome.

Reducing an emission flow changes the rate at which atmospheric carbon accumulates. Actual atmospheric decline depends on the balance between anthropogenic inputs and removal by land, ocean, and other pathways. That distinction is why stock–flow thinking is useful.

Published context

Recent sinks absorb roughly half of anthropogenic CO₂.

The Global Carbon Budget 2025 reports that over 2015–2024, land absorbed about 21% and ocean about 29% of anthropogenic CO₂ emissions. The public demo uses these figures only as context for the system structure.
