Structural result

Short aggregate inertia is supported; a literal long capital lifetime is not.

Forcing τ into the 15–35 year physical-turnover prior puts the optimum on the 15-year lower boundary. The fossil-share proxy therefore does not behave like a direct measurement of one long-lived installed-capital stock.

Allowing τ to represent effective aggregate adjustment yields an interior 3-year optimum. The dynamic fit also beats the best static/no-inertia model, so some inertia is supported by training data.
