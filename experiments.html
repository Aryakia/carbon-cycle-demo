What changes when the policy—or the history—changes?

These experiments hold the model structure fixed and vary explicit policy packages, timing, institutional resistance, or initial conditions. They are designed to expose inertia and pathway dependence generated by the model equations. They are not forecasts, causal estimates, or evidence that every energy system behaves the same way.

What is not claimed

These are structural experiments, not calibrated policy forecasts.

  • Behavioral coefficients are not globally estimated.
  • The natural-sink closure remains pedagogical.
  • No dollar transition cost is shown because a defensible cost module has not yet been calibrated.
  • Differences across cases are consequences of this model structure and parameterization.
