# Carbon Cycle Demo

**An interactive conceptual stock–flow view of carbon moving through the Earth system.**

**Repository type:** Public interactive demo  
**Project area:** Carbon cycle · Earth systems · System Dynamics · systems thinking  
**Status:** Exploratory conceptual prototype

---

## Purpose

This project explores how climate change and carbon can be understood as a **stock-and-flow system** rather than only as an atmospheric CO₂ concentration.

The demo organizes the carbon system around major reservoirs such as the atmosphere, oceans, terrestrial biosphere, soils, and geological/fossil stocks, then shows the major transfer pathways that connect them.

## Why this framing

Atmospheric CO₂ is one observable state of a much larger carbon system. A stock–flow perspective helps connect emissions, photosynthesis, respiration, ocean uptake and release, land-use change, burial, weathering, and geological processes in one coherent structure.

## Demo design

The public page is intentionally conceptual. Visitors can select major carbon reservoirs and inspect the kinds of flows that connect them.

It does **not** claim to be a calibrated Earth-system model, and the current public version does not use the interface to make quantitative climate forecasts.

## Major conceptual stocks

- Atmosphere
- Surface and deep ocean
- Terrestrial biomass
- Soils
- Fossil/geological carbon

## Example flows represented

- Photosynthesis
- Respiration and decomposition
- Air–sea exchange
- Fossil-fuel combustion
- Land-use change
- Sedimentation and burial
- Weathering and geological release

## Next research direction

The longer-term goal is to connect this visual structure to published reservoir estimates, historical emissions, ocean and terrestrial uptake, methane oxidation, feedbacks, and long-timescale geological flows.
