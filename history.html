Stage 11 · observations before calibration

What historical behavior must the model explain?

This page defines the empirical reference layer for the upstream model. The observed series are kept distinct from model stocks: renewable capacity is a proxy for clean capital, total primary-energy supply provides the preferred historical aggregate activity driver, fossil-energy consumption is decomposed into energy activity and fossil dependency, and GDP remains a secondary macroeconomic diagnostic.

Decision:

Do not run an unconstrained multi-parameter optimization yet. The current data strongly constrain fossil-flow behavior and provide useful clean/fossil activity proxies, but production-capacity investment and latent institutional/capability feedbacks remain under-observed.

leakage rule

2018–2025 stays held out

Validation observations cannot be used for parameter fitting, prior selection, or objective-weight tuning. If model structure is changed after inspecting holdout results, the research record must explicitly say the holdout was consumed.

shock metadata

2008–09 and 2020 are marked

The global financial crisis and COVID-19 are recorded as external disturbance windows. They are not automatically excluded or down-weighted; any treatment must be explicit in a calibration run.

optimizer gate

No free multi-parameter fit yet

Stage 12 shows substantial local confounding. The next requirement is a historical/backcast engine plus stronger capacity/investment observations—not a larger optimizer.

Atmospheric carbon

Initialized from the end-1999 state of the separately calibrated reduced carbon-cycle replay.

explicitly latent

Capacity, capability, institutions

Historical initialization refuses to run unless production capacity, accumulated fossil capability, and institutional support are supplied explicitly. They are not hidden in 2025 defaults.

Remaining calibration gate:

A historical optimizer still should not be run freely across these latent initial states and all formation/decay coefficients. Stage 12 showed strong confounding. Capacity/investment evidence or a deliberately reduced fossil-side subsystem is still required.
