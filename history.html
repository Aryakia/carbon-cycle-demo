Stage 11 · observations before calibration

What historical behavior must the model explain?

This page defines the empirical reference layer for the upstream model. The observed series are kept distinct from model stocks: renewable capacity is a proxy for clean capital, fossil-energy consumption is a proxy for demand activity, and GDP is a candidate driver—not a parameter imposed by assumption.
